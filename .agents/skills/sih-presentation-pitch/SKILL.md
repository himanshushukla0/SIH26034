# Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Winning Pitch & Deck Strategy

This skill provides the structure, rubrics, and engineering narratives to maximize evaluation scores from SIH judges and Ministry of Consumer Affairs officials.

## SIH Evaluation Rubric Weightage

1. **Novelty & Technical Innovation (25%):**
   - Multi-agent architecture combining spatial OCR, cylindrical unwarping, zero-shot multimodal verification, and automated USP calculation.
   - Dual-engine auditing: Physical packaging scanning + Automated e-commerce crawling.

2. **Feasibility & Architecture (25%):**
   - End-to-end operational pipeline: edge-friendly camera scanning, cloud-scalable worker queues, automated report dispatch.
   - High precision metrics: low false positive rate, robust against real-world package glare, creases, and wear.

3. **Impact on Ministry & Society (20%):**
   - Eliminates manual inspection bottlenecks for Legal Metrology officers.
   - Protects 1.4B Indian consumers from hidden price hikes, missing expiry dates, and deceptive online product claims.

4. **Commercialization & Adoption Potential (15%):**
   - Readily integrable as a plug-in for state Legal Metrology departments, ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce), and major FMCG compliance teams before product dispatch.

5. **Clarity of Presentation & Live Demonstration (15%):**
   - Live interactive scanning demonstration with real-time bounding boxes, rule checklist ticking off live, and immediate downloadable infraction notice.

## Standard SIH Deck Slide Sequence (10 Slides)

- **Slide 1: Title & Team Credentials** (Problem ID: SIH26034, Ministry, Solution Name, Tagline).
- **Slide 2: The Problem Statement & Market Reality** (Trillions of packages, manual inspection failure, non-compliant e-commerce listings).
- **Slide 3: Regulatory Foundation** (Legal Metrology Act 2009, LMPC 2011 Rules, 2021/2022 USP and E-commerce mandates).
- **Slide 4: Proposed Solution & Value Proposition** (Autonomous Multi-Agent LMPC Compliance Engine).
- **Slide 5: Technical Architecture & Agent Workflow** (Ingestion -> Vision Preprocessing -> Multimodal OCR -> Rule Engine -> Dashboard).
- **Slide 6: Innovation & Key Differentiators** (Physical font height measurement in mm, cylindrical de-warping, cross-modal image vs spec auditor).
- **Slide 7: Live Demo & Product Screenshots** (Auditing interface, real-time bounding boxes, infraction notices).
- **Slide 8: Feasibility, Tech Stack & Scalability** (Python, FastAPI, OpenCV, PaddleOCR, LLM Vision, React/Vanilla JS).
- **Slide 9: Impact, Government Benefits & Future Roadmap** (ONDC integration, mobile app for field officers, consumer grievance auto-link).
- **Slide 10: Conclusion & Q&A** (Summary and call to action).
